# Project Idea

While exploring existing AI resume optimization tools, I noticed a common problem.

Most tools improve the ATS score by adding:
- Fake skills
- Fake certifications
- Fake projects
- Fake achievements
- Technologies that are not actually present in the user's resume

This may help pass ATS screening, but it creates problems during interviews because candidates may be asked about things they never actually did.



 My Innovation

CareerPilot AI follows a **Truthful Resume Optimization** approach.

Instead of creating a completely new resume, it improves the existing one while keeping every detail genuine.

CareerPilot AI:
-  Uses only the information already available in the resume.
-  Improves grammar and professional wording.
-  Makes the resume ATS-friendly.
-  Identifies missing keywords from the job description.
-  Explains why every suggestion is made.
-  Generates interview questions based on the user's resume and job description.

CareerPilot AI never:
-  Adds fake skills.
-  Adds fake certifications.
-  Adds fake projects.
-  Adds fake work experience.
-  Adds fake achievements.
-  Changes education or personal information.

---

# What Makes CareerPilot AI Different?

Most resume tools only provide an ATS score or generate a completely new resume.

CareerPilot AI goes one step further by:
- Comparing the resume with the job description.
- Showing matched and missing skills.
- Giving ATS and keyword match scores.
- Explaining every improvement made by AI.
- Rewriting the resume professionally without changing the truth.
- Helping users prepare for interviews with personalized AI-generated questions.

---

# Goal

The goal of CareerPilot AI is not just to increase an ATS score.

It is to help job seekers build **honest, professional, and recruiter-friendly resumes** while understanding how they can improve their chances of getting shortlisted.
